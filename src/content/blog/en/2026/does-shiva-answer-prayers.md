### Silver

I look at it this way: It never hurts to try.

Manifestation is the power of Shakti, and I personally advocate for developing a devotional energy and relationship to both Shiva and Shakti, and to take turns inhabiting their energy.

For instance, take some time to sit inside Shiva's energy then feel your Shakti manifestation as the whole of the world around you – every object you look upon, every sight you see, every smell, taste, touch, and sound. The ALL of it. Cultivate that perspective of appreciation for Her giving you a body to have this experience, and inject sacred reverence directly into your daily life.

You can pretend you're praying to your team of light, to Shakti, to your higher self... It's your game! You get to make believe to your heart's content and be as a child in his wonder. Every cast and supporting character will be there as your Shakti, dressed up just to play with you.

The wonderful absurdity of this life is that it's all "make believe" – and that that IS the point of it... That's why they call it the Lila, the Divine Play. So it doesn't matter how much or how little you get into your 'act' or 'character' – it's all You anyway.

And if Shakti wants to let little ego play with her phenomenal cosmic powers that literally warp, create, and bring forth all of reality across all of infinity – across every plane of existence, every world, every dimension... who's stopping her?

Part of the wild freedom of Shakti is the eternal YES – the fact that no one gets to constrain her in her infinite creativity.

So, does prayer work? Yes, and also no...

It depends on who you are and the game you're playing. But if you feel called toward it, then the answer is likely yes. And even when Shakti is playing at being a Christian and praying to Jesus and it seems to work, that too is part of her YES.

That is why KS Tantra is often considered one of the most inclusive frameworks from which to see reality – because of the absurd truth of Infinity, and what it means for Shiva and Shakti to be pure, unbridled potential of every possible seemingness.

*(Smiles, takes a moment to sit in his own Shiva energy and feel the body of his Beloved, the Intimacy of it, how close she is, the lack of distance...)* – Enjoy the Play my friend. 🍷🌹
